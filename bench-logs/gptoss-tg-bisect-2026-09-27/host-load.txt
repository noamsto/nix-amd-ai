Context. The machine runs several sibling dispatcher workers (pi/claude
processes); during the 18:53-19:02 window the 1-minute load average was ~72 on
32 cores. The two rounds taken in that window (r7, r8) are the ones whose model
load wall time is ~2x the other rounds (see each phase's timeline.txt) and whose
throughput drops to ~40 t/s vs ~53 t/s in the later low-load rounds.

The per-run wall times in the committed `timeline.txt` files are the per-run
load proxy; the samples above bracket the spike and the recovery.
